ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS
================================================================================

WHY QUERIES FAIL:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Column-Table Mismatch (43.8% of errors)
   Problem: LLM uses columns that don't exist in specific tables
   Examples:
   • bowler__fullname in fixtures__bowling (only in fixtures__balls)
   • 'score' in fixtures__balls (should be 'score__runs')
   • 'overs' in fixtures__balls (should be 'ball')

2. GROUP BY Violations (25% of errors)
   Problem: SELECT columns not in GROUP BY or aggregate
   Cause: SQL Server strict GROUP BY rules not fully enforced

3. Subquery Issues (12.5% of errors)
   Problem: Subqueries returning multiple rows
   Cause: Missing TOP 1 or aggregate functions

WHY ANSWERS ARE MEANINGLESS:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Overly Specific Conditions (90% of meaningless)
   • Combining rare scenarios (e.g., "10 wickets + 100+ runs")
   • Specific team + venue + condition combinations
   • Historical queries where data doesn't exist

2. Query Logic Issues (10% of meaningless)
   • Correct syntax but flawed logic
   • Wrong JOIN conditions filter out all data
